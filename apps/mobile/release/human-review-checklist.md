# Human release review checklist

This checklist is intentionally not auto-approved. A named owner must review and record evidence before any internal test upload or store decision.

- [ ] Legal publisher identity, institutional naming, disclaimer and app identity are correct.
- [ ] Rights and licences for ManualSAMUR content, attachments, icons, fonts, map/provider material and screenshots are documented.
- [ ] Privacy and data declarations match the shipped behavior: no account, patient data collection, required analytics, or cross-device sync.
- [ ] Support URL, privacy URL, official-source URL, contact details and platform declarations are live and correct.
- [ ] Spanish store metadata, title, subtitle/short description, full description, keywords/category and age/content declarations are reviewed.
- [ ] Screenshots are current, synthetic, accessible, free of PHI, and match the signed candidate on both platforms.
- [ ] Reviewer instructions explain offline behavior, first-use disclosure, attachment fallback, location denial fallback and the independent/non-official identity.
- [ ] Spanish release notes describe only shipped, evidenced behavior and disclose any known limitations.
- [ ] The owner reviews the evidence matrix, signs the candidate handoff, and records the TestFlight/Google Play internal-test decision.
- [ ] Submission, rollout, pause/halt, rollback and production approval remain explicit human decisions.
